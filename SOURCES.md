## Lead — Strait of Hormuz tankers

- Three oil tankers exploded and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz
  after hitting naval mines, Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC)
  Navy said in a statement on Monday night. (PressTV, 23 Nov 22:20
  GMT = 24 Nov 05:20 WIB; Al Jazeera live blog, 24 Nov 00:00 UK;
  Jerusalem Post, 24 Nov 00:23 UTC; JBizNews, 24 Nov 00:15 UTC)
- The IRGC said the tankers were "violating" vessels that had
  ignored navigational warnings; independent verification of the
  ships' identities, damage and crews was not available at the time.
  (Newsy Today / WION, 24 Nov; Al Jazeera, 24 Nov)
- The image says "Ships hit naval mines, Iran's Guard says" — the
  claim is attributed, not stated as independently confirmed fact.
